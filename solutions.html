Revenue Performance Benchmarking™

Know exactly where your restaurant stands in its market, and what to do about it. Revenue Performance Benchmarking compares your results against a competitive set you choose, then turns the gaps into a clear, prioritised plan.

  • Benchmark the numbers that matter. Occupancy, Average Check and RevPAS, each indexed against your market (MPI, ACI and RGI), with your rank in the competitive set.
  • Compare like with like. Build your competitive set by restaurant type, cuisine, segment, suburb or specific venues.
  • See the full picture. Compare by service (breakfast, lunch, dinner), period (week, month, quarter and year to date), weekday or weekend, events such as Valentine's Day or the AFL Final, and weather.
  • Track your trends. Follow your performance over time against the previous period and the same time last year.
  • Turn insight into action. AI-led SWOT analysis and strategic planning produce a priority action plan, and you can track progress and revenue reclaimed each week.

Web Performance Benchmarking™

Find out whether your website is winning or losing customers compared with your competitors. Web Performance Benchmarking compares your digital performance with the market and industry segment, and shows where to focus.

  • Compare your traffic. Sessions, unique visitors and page views, side by side with your competitive set's average.
  • Understand where visitors come from. Organic search, direct, email, paid and social channels, plus new versus returning visitors, device mix and busiest days.
  • Get plain-language insights. For example: “77% of your sessions are from mobile, so optimise for mobile.”
  • Plan your digital strategy. AI-led SWOT analysis and an action plan based on your web benchmarks.
